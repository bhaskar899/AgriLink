About AgriLink

👨‍🌾 How AgriLink Empowers Farmers

AgriLink helps farmers sell directly to retailers without middlemen, ensuring fair prices and transparent business.

  • 🌾 Upload fresh products easily
  • 💰 Set your own fair price
  • 📦 Accept & manage retailer orders
  • 📍 Track delivery progress live
  • 🔔 Get instant notifications

🚛 Driver Picks Up Orders

After a retailer places an order, a verified AgriLink driver collects products from the farmer with complete tracking and status updates.

  • 📍 Gets pickup location instantly
  • 🧾 Driver receives order details automatically
  • 👨‍🔧 Verified & trained delivery partner
  • 🔄 Status updates: Picked → On the Way
  • 📸 Can upload delivery proof images

🏪 Driver Delivers to Retailer

The driver safely delivers the product to the retailer with full monitoring, ensuring timely and secure handover.Retailer Can Negotiate With Farmer And Also Do Real Time Payment For Buy A Product.

  • 🛣 Real-time route tracking
  • Do Real Time Payment For Buy A Product
  • ✔ Retailer receives live updates
  • 📦 Smooth & secure handover
  • 📲 Retailer can track driver location
  • 🎯 Fast, safe, and reliable delivery
