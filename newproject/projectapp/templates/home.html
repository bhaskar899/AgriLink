🌿 How It Works

About AgriLink

A transparent supply chain that connects farmers directly to retailers — no middlemen, fair prices, real-time tracking.

👨‍🌾

How AgriLink Empowers Farmers

Sell directly to retailers without middlemen, ensuring fair prices and transparent business.

  • 🌾 Upload fresh products easily
  • 💰 Set your own fair price
  • 📦 Accept & manage retailer orders
  • 📍 Track delivery progress live
  • 🔔 Get instant notifications
🚛

Driver Picks Up Orders

After a retailer places an order, a verified AgriLink driver collects products from the farmer with complete tracking.

  • 📍 Gets pickup location instantly
  • 🧾 Receives order details automatically
  • 👨‍🔧 Verified & trained delivery partner
  • 🔄 Status: Picked → On the Way
  • 📸 Uploads delivery proof images
🏪

Driver Delivers to Retailer

The driver safely delivers products to the retailer with full monitoring. Retailers can negotiate prices and do real-time payments.

  • 🛣️ Real-time route tracking
  • 💳 Real-time payment support
  • ✔️ Retailer gets live updates
  • 📦 Smooth & secure handover
  • 🎯 Fast, safe, reliable delivery
