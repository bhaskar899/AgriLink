About AgriLink

AgriLink is a smart agriculture platform built to connect farmers directly with retailers. Farmers can upload product details, set prices, and track orders. Retailers can browse products, place orders, and track deliveries — all from one platform.

  • 🚜 Direct farmer-to-retailer trading
  • 📍 Live product tracking
  • 📦 Transparent order system
  • 💰 Fair pricing without middlemen
